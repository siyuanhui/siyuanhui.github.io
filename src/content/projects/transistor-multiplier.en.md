## Problem and objective

Designing a combinational 4×4 unsigned multiplier with an 8-bit output at the transistor-schematic level. The work compares partial-product reduction methods, adder structures, and transistor sizing against an energy–delay–area objective, E<sub>avg</sub>·D<sub>max</sub>²·A, under a specified supply and load, with area measured as total effective MOSFET width.

## Testing and verification

Planned evaluation includes exhaustive functional checks across all 256 input pairs, critical-path transition tests, and energy and worst-case delay simulations.
