Projects

In progress Fall 2026

Transistor-Level 4×4 Unsigned Multiplier

EECS 312 · Cadence · IBM 130 nm CMOS PDK

Designing a combinational 4×4 unsigned multiplier with an 8-bit output at the transistor-schematic level. The work compares partial-product reduction methods, adder structures, and transistor sizing against an energy–delay–area objective, Eavg·Dmax²·A, under a specified supply and load, with area measured as total effective MOSFET width.

Planned evaluation includes exhaustive functional checks across all 256 input pairs, critical-path transition tests, and energy and worst-case delay simulations.

In progress Fall 2026

LC2K Processor Toolchain & Architecture Simulation

EECS 370 · C · LC2K assembly

Built an assembler and ISA simulator in C, then extended the toolchain with object-file generation, symbol resolution, relocation, and multi-file linking. Currently developing a cycle-accurate five-stage pipeline model with pipeline registers, data forwarding, load-use stalls, and predict-not-taken branches.

Scope includes a unified instruction/data cache simulator with configurable block size and associativity, LRU replacement, and write-back / write-allocate policy, plus directed assembly tests for hazards and cache behavior.

March–May 2025

Embedded Robotics & Hardware Integration

Hardware Developer & Team Lead · SJTU Science & Technology Association · C/C++

Worked on an embedded robotic platform and manipulator with joystick control, integrating microcontroller firmware, motor actuation, and hardware interfaces. Wrote control firmware in C/C++ and coordinated hardware debugging and integration.
